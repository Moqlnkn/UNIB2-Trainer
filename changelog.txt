SF6 FDM Colors:
Green = Counter-Hittable (startup)
Blue = Punish-Counterable (recovery)
Cyan = Non-Counterable (dashes, jumps, etc)
Red = Active
Orange = Active projectile
Purple = Parrying
Yellow = Hitstun/Blockstun
Gray w/white stripes = Full Invincible
Red w/white stripes = Strike+Throw Invincible (Level 1 Supers)
Orange w/white stripes = Projectile Invincible
Red(top) Orange(bot) w/white stripes = Strike/Projectile Invincible
